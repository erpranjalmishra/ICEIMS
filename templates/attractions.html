Travel Tips

Make the most of your visit to Delhi NCR

Metro Connectivity

Delhi Metro provides excellent connectivity to most tourist attractions. The Blue Line connects to Greater Noida.

Best Time to Visit

November weather is pleasant with comfortable temperatures, perfect for sightseeing and outdoor activities.

Mobile Apps

Download Delhi Metro app, Uber/Ola for transportation, and Google Translate for language assistance.

Transportation Options

Taxi/Cab

Uber, Ola, or hotel arranged cabs for convenient door-to-door service.

Delhi Metro

Most economical and efficient way to travel within Delhi. Blue Line serves Greater Noida.

DTC Buses

Delhi Transport Corporation buses connect various parts of the city at low cost.

Auto Rickshaw

Traditional three-wheelers for short distances and local transportation.
