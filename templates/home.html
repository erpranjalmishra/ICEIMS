Conference Objective

1
Promote Knowledge Exchange

Provide a platform for presenting cutting-edge research findings, case studies, and practical applications in sustainable energy and advanced manufacturing systems.

2
Foster Interdisciplinary Collaboration

Encourage interaction between experts from energy, manufacturing, automation, and artificial intelligence domains to address global industrial and environmental challenges.

3
Highlight Sustainable Practices

Explore strategies for clean energy integration, resource optimization, and environmentally responsible manufacturing.

4
Showcase Technological Innovations

Present recent advancements in smart manufacturing, Industry 4.0, robotics, digital twins, and intelligent energy management.

5
Strengthen Academia–Industry Linkages

Facilitate partnerships and collaborations between universities, research institutions, and industries for future projects and innovation.

6
Inspire Future Research

Motivate young researchers and professionals to engage in innovative work that contributes to energy efficiency, smart production systems, and sustainable industrial development.
