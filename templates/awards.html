Award Categories

ICEIMS 2026 proudly presents multiple award categories to recognize and honor exceptional research contributions, innovation, and academic excellence in the field of Energy and Intelligent Manufacturing Systems.

Best Paper Award

Excellence in Research

Recognizes the most outstanding research paper with exceptional innovation, methodology, and significant contribution to the field.

₹25,000
+ Certificate + Trophy
Selection Criteria:
  • Novelty and Innovation
  • Technical Quality
  • Presentation Excellence
  • Impact on Field

Young Researcher Award

Next Generation Excellence

Honors outstanding research contributions by young researchers under 35 years, encouraging the future leaders in science and engineering.

₹15,000
+ Certificate + Trophy
Eligibility:
  • Age below 35 years
  • First author of paper
  • Student or Early Career
  • Exceptional quality

Innovation Award

Creative Solutions

Celebrates the most innovative solution or technology with significant potential for real-world application and industry impact.

₹20,000
+ Certificate + Trophy
Focus Areas:
  • Practical Innovation
  • Industry Applications
  • Commercial Potential
  • Sustainability Impact

Best Presentation Award

Communication Excellence

Recognizes exceptional presentation skills, clear communication of complex ideas, and engaging delivery during conference sessions.

₹10,000
+ Certificate + Trophy
Evaluation Criteria:

  • Visual Design Quality
  • Audience Engagement
  • Q&A Handling

Outstanding Poster Award

Special Recognition Award

Honors exceptional contributions to conference activities, outstanding collaboration, or significant community service in the field.

  • Community Service
  • Collaborative Research

Award Selection Process

1
Nomination

Papers are automatically considered based on peer review scores and committee recommendations.

2
Evaluation

Expert jury panel evaluates nominations based on established criteria and conference presentations.

3
Recognition

Awards are announced during the closing ceremony with trophy presentation and certificates.

Important Information

Eligibility Requirements

All registered conference participants with accepted papers are automatically eligible for awards consideration.

Award Ceremony

Winners will be announced during the closing ceremony on November 22, 2026.

Recognition

All winners receive physical trophies, certificates, and cash prizes as mentioned.

Selection Committee

Awards are selected by a distinguished panel of international experts and conference committee members.
