Conference Overview

ICEIMS 2026 is a premier international conference that brings together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students to share knowledge and discuss the latest developments in Energy and Intelligent Manufacturing Systems.

The conference aims to foster collaboration between academia and industry, promoting innovative solutions for sustainable manufacturing processes and intelligent energy systems. With a focus on cutting-edge research and practical applications, ICEIMS 2026 serves as a platform for knowledge exchange and networking among global experts.
