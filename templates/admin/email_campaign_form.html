What is a Campaign?

Email campaigns allow you to send emails to multiple recipients at once using a pre-defined template.

Recipient Filters:
  • All Users: Send to everyone
  • Reviewers: Only paper reviewers
  • Authors: Only paper authors
  • Admins: Only admin staff
  • Attendees: Only registered attendees
