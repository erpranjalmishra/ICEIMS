Conference Awards

Recognizing excellence in research and innovation

Celebrating outstanding contributions to Energy and Intelligent Manufacturing Systems

Best Paper Award

Recognizes the most outstanding research paper presented at the conference, judged on innovation, methodology, and contribution to the field.

Prize: ₹25,000 + Certificate + Trophy
Selection Criteria:
  • Novelty and Innovation
  • Technical Quality
  • Presentation Excellence
  • Impact on Field

Young Researcher Award

Honors outstanding research contributions by young researchers under 35 years, encouraging the next generation of scientists and engineers.

Prize: ₹15,000 + Certificate + Trophy
Eligibility:
  • Age below 35 years
  • First author of the paper
  • Student or Early Career Researcher
  • Exceptional research quality

Innovation Award

Celebrates the most innovative solution or technology presented, with potential for real-world application and industry impact.

Prize: ₹20,000 + Certificate + Trophy
Focus Areas:
  • Practical Innovation
  • Industry Applications
  • Commercialization Potential
  • Societal Impact

Lifetime Achievement Award

Honors distinguished researchers for their lifelong contributions to Energy and Intelligent Manufacturing Systems research and education.

Recognition: Gold Medal + Certificate + Special Citation
Recognition for:
  • Decades of Research Excellence
  • Mentorship and Teaching
  • Industry Leadership
  • Global Impact

Award Selection Process

1
Nomination

Papers are automatically considered based on review scores and presentation quality

2
Evaluation

Expert panel reviews nominated works using established criteria

3
Selection

Awards committee makes final selections through consensus

4
Ceremony

Winners are announced and honored during the closing ceremony
