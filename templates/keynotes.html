Leading researchers and industry pioneers in Energy and Intelligent Manufacturing Systems

Dr. Sarah Johnson

Professor of Energy Systems

Massachusetts Institute of Technology, USA

"Sustainable Energy Solutions for Industry 4.0"

Dr. Johnson will explore the integration of renewable energy systems with intelligent manufacturing processes, discussing breakthrough technologies and their impact on sustainable industrial development.

Day 1 - Opening Ceremony 9:00 AM - 10:00 AM
Biography

Dr. Sarah Johnson is a renowned expert in sustainable energy systems with over 20 years of experience. She has published 150+ papers and holds 25 patents in energy optimization and smart manufacturing technologies.

Prof. Hiroshi Tanaka

Director of AI Research Center

Tokyo Institute of Technology, Japan

"AI-Driven Manufacturing: The Future is Now"

Prof. Tanaka will present cutting-edge AI applications in manufacturing, including predictive maintenance, quality control, and autonomous production systems transforming modern industry.

Day 2 - Technical Session 11:30 AM - 12:30 PM
Biography

Prof. Tanaka is a pioneer in artificial intelligence applications for manufacturing. He leads multiple international research projects and has received numerous awards for his contributions to smart manufacturing.

Dr. Maria Rodriguez

Chief Innovation Officer

Siemens Digital Industries, Germany

"Digital Twins in Smart Manufacturing"

Dr. Rodriguez will demonstrate how digital twin technology is revolutionizing manufacturing processes, enabling real-time optimization and predictive analytics.

Day 2 - Industry Panel 2:00 PM - 3:00 PM
Biography

Dr. Rodriguez leads digital transformation initiatives at Siemens, focusing on Industry 4.0 solutions. She has extensive experience in implementing smart manufacturing systems across global industries.

Prof. David Chen

Chair of Sustainable Engineering

Stanford University, USA

"Green Manufacturing: Challenges and Opportunities"

Prof. Chen will address environmental challenges in manufacturing and present innovative approaches to achieve sustainable production while maintaining efficiency and quality.

Day 3 - Closing Session 10:00 AM - 11:00 AM
Biography

Prof. Chen is a leading authority on sustainable manufacturing practices. His research focuses on environmental impact reduction and circular economy principles in industrial processes.
