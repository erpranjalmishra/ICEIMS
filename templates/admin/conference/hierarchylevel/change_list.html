How to Use Hierarchy Levels

Hierarchy levels organize your committee members into different categories. Lower level numbers appear first on the committee page.

  • Add New Level: Click "Add Hierarchy Level" button above to create a new category (e.g., "Advisory Board", "Student Committee", etc.)
  • Edit Level Names: Click on any level name in the list below to change it directly
  • Level Numbers: Use sequential numbers (1, 2, 3...). Lower numbers appear first
  • Delete Levels: You can only delete levels that have no members assigned
  • Active/Inactive: Toggle the active status to hide/show levels without deleting them

💡 Tip: After adding a new level, go to "Committee Members" to assign people to it.
